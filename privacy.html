Privacy Policy – Auto #1

Last updated: October 9, 2026

1. Who we are

Auto #1 ("the App") is a personal desktop tool used by a single creator to upload his own videos to his own TikTok account.

2. Data we access

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) via the user.info.basic scope, to confirm which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, to upload or publish videos when requested (video.upload, video.publish).

3. How data is stored

Tokens and profile data are stored only locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics. No servers operated by the App collect user data.

4. Retention and deletion

Data is kept only as long as the TikTok authorization is active. Revoking access from TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions) and deleting the local token file removes all data.

5. Contact

Questions: open an issue on this project's GitHub page.

Résumé en français : L'app accède uniquement au profil de base et aux jetons TikTok nécessaires pour publier ses propres vidéos. Les données restent sur l'ordinateur du propriétaire, ne sont ni vendues ni partagées, et sont supprimées en révoquant l'accès.
